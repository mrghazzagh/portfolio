CourseMaker.

Making it easier for faculty to build clear, consistent, interactive pages in Brightspace.

FOCUSFaculty tools · accessibility · Brightspace
SETTINGMcLennan Community College
BUILD
The story behind the work

The challenge

Faculty need course pages that are engaging and accessible, but building them from scratch in an LMS editor takes time. A useful solution had to fit the real constraints of Brightspace content pages and be approachable for instructors who do not write HTML.

What I built

I developed CourseMaker, a browser-based page builder with reusable blocks such as accordions, tabs, knowledge checks, guided steppers, decision scenarios, callouts, and timelines. Faculty can preview a block, customize its content, and copy the generated page markup into Brightspace.

The design decisions

The interface organizes blocks into categories and provides previews so instructors can recognize a pattern before using it. The output is designed around the limited styling and scripting available within Brightspace. Companion support pages and step-by-step guides connect the tool to the faculty workflow.

Why it matters

This project is an example of turning repeated one-to-one support into a reusable resource. The goal is to give faculty a faster starting point while preserving room for their voice and content.

A note on evidence

The live tool is a working example. Specific adoption and learning outcome metrics are not claimed here.
