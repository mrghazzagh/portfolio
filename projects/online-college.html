The story behind the work

The challenge

Scaling online learning involves more than adding courses. Students need a coherent experience across classes, and faculty need clear expectations, ownership, and support.

The work in progress

I am contributing to MCC’s Online College planning through discussions about master course shells, minimum instructional content, accessibility, course review, and the roles of faculty and support staff. The model calls for shared standards that apply to Online College courses and room for collaborative decisions by the faculty who teach them.

The system behind a course

A sustainable structure connects design, teaching, advising, technical support, and quality review. I focus on making those handoffs visible so a course can be maintained and improved over time.

Leadership lens

This work draws on team coordination, faculty consultation, and practical change management. The goal is to build consistency that supports learning while making expectations understandable for the people doing the work.

A note on evidence
