The story behind the work

The challenge

A course can contain all its required materials and still leave students uncertain about sequence, expectations, and support. Faculty also need a structure they can maintain through the term.

A repeatable learning rhythm

My work with online course templates emphasizes a weekly overview, clear objectives and task list, concise concept support, meaningful practice, and visible assessment expectations. Those pieces help students move from orientation to action within each module.

Preparing faculty

At MCC, I support faculty through course review and professional development, including Teaching Online Fundamentals. The six-week experience covers teacher presence, accessibility, online course design, engagement, assessment, and a final course-building project.

Quality as a partnership

The Principles of Good Practice review brings instructors, instructional designers, supervisors, and academic leadership into a shared quality process. My role is to help translate standards into decisions that work in a specific course and discipline.

A note on evidence

Course examples may contain faculty-owned content or student information, so this public case study focuses on process and structure.
