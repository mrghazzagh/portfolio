DESIGN CHALLENGE / 01The situation
✳

A faculty member says students keep missing assignments in their online course.

The weekly module has a lecture video, a PDF, a discussion, a quiz, and a link to an outside homework system. What would you investigate first?

THE DESIGN LENS

I’d walk the student path with the instructor, check where tasks and due dates appear, and look for points where navigation or expectations break down. Then we could test a clearer weekly overview and consistent activity placement. Reminders may help, but the course structure should do its share of the work.
