About

Learning is a design problem.
I solve it.

I'm an instructional designer, educator, and AI strategist based in Waco, Texas. I lead the Instructional Design team at McLennan Community College, where I work at the intersection of pedagogy, technology, and human-centered design.

As chair of MCC's AI Task Force, I help faculty and staff navigate the shift toward AI literacy -- not through policing, but through building real understanding. My work spans course design, professional development, accessibility, and the future of online learning.

First-generation college graduate. Master's in Instructional Design. Lifelong learner. Occasional podcaster on MAC Talks.

Mission

"Learning should be designed with the same intention and craft as any great experience. My job is to make sure it is."

I believe every learner deserves a thoughtfully designed experience -- one that respects their time, meets them where they are, and actually moves them forward. That's not a platitude. It's an operational principle that drives every course, every training, every initiative I touch.

At MCC, that means building systems for quality, not just individual courses. It means training faculty to think like designers. It means treating AI as a tool for access and equity, not a shortcut or a threat. And it means never losing sight of the humans on the other end of the screen.
